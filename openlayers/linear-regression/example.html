This monitoring report tracks vegetation health trends at Tindarey from 2017-04-09 – 2026-04-16. Using 566 Sentinel-2 satellite observations grouped into 104 monthly snapshots, the analysis mapped how plant greenness (NDVI) changed across 2657.79 ha over this period. Greening trends indicate vegetation recovery or growth; declining trends may flag areas of concern. Detailed imagery and technical parameters are provided in the sections below.
