About

Geospatial and nature-data leader who helps organisations understand, monitor and report their impact on nature. I've delivered nature-risk, restoration and carbon work for global corporates and financial institutions, and built monitoring services used across a global restoration platform. Previously, at the European Commission's Joint Research Centre, I co-led the Global Surface Water dataset, published in Nature and adopted as the official UN SDG indicator. I bring rare depth across science, policy, business and cloud architecture, with experience spanning government, academia, international NGOs and startups.

I'm currently Head of Geospatial at Restor and available for remote consultancy, contract, interim and fractional work, from the UK, with clients worldwide.

Services

Nature and deforestation risk assessment

Helping companies and investors see where their supply chains and sites touch deforestation, biodiversity loss or climate risk, and what to do about it.

Nature reporting indicators

Building credible, data-backed metrics for TNFD and other nature-related disclosures.

Restoration and carbon monitoring

Independent satellite and drone-based evidence that projects are delivering, for developers, funders and carbon buyers.

Geospatial data products and services

Designing and building scalable products such as subscription monitoring services, APIs and automated reporting, using Earth Engine, Google Cloud and cloud-native geospatial technologies.

Selected work

Supply chain nature risk for global corporates (ADM, Amazon)

Challenge: Understand where operations and supply chains touch deforestation and biodiversity risk.

Outcome: Assessed risk across sites and supply chains, and identified practical ways to reduce it, including sites suitable for restoration.

Climate risk for a global bank (BNP Paribas)

Challenge: Understand exposure of agricultural supply chain sites in Brazil to future climate change.

Outcome: Assessed flood risk to those sites under future climate scenarios.

Nature reporting for an insurer and asset manager (Manulife)

Challenge: Need credible metrics to report biodiversity impact under TNFD.

Outcome: Developed State of Nature indicators that companies can use for TNFD reporting.

Restoration and carbon assurance (Tahi, New Zealand)

Challenge: Provide independent evidence that former farmland is being restored to forest.

Outcome: Delivered evidence of tree growth, biomass and carbon from drone and lidar data.

XPRIZE Rainforest finalist

Challenge: Monitor rainforest biodiversity at scale, fast enough for a global competition.

Outcome: Part of Restor's team, one of six finalists at the 2024 final in Manaus, Brazil.

Platform trust at global scale (Restor)

Challenge: Give funders and partners confidence in restoration claims across a global platform.

Outcome: Led automated verification of more than 350,000 restoration sites.

New subscription revenue (Restor)

Challenge: Give customers ongoing assurance that their sites remain undisturbed.

Outcome: Launched a monitoring service plus vegetation change reporting for partner Accounting for Nature.

Global Surface Water (European Commission JRC)

Challenge: Produce a global, long-term record of surface water for science and policy.

Outcome: Co-led a dataset published in Nature and adopted as the official indicator for UN SDG 6.6.1.
